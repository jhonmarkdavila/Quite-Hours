Baluarte de San Diego

About Baluarte de San Diego

Baluarte de San Diego is one of the places I recommend for people who want to explore a peaceful and historic side of Intramuros. The old stone structures and open surroundings make it a nice place to slow down and appreciate the history of the area. You can walk around, take some photos, enjoy the surroundings, and learn more about the past of Manila. It is a simple place to visit, but the old walls and ruins give it a unique atmosphere that makes the experience more memorable.

Place Details

Location Santa Lucia St. cor. Muralla St., Intramuros, Manila
Type Historic Fortification and Archaeological Park
Entrance Fee ₱75 regular / ₱50 discounted
Best Time to Visit Morning or late afternoon
Recommended Activity Walking, sightseeing, photography, and exploring history
