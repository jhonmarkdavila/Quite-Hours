Fort Santiago

About Fort Santiago

Fort Santiago is one of the places I recommend for people who want to explore history while enjoying a relaxing walk around Intramuros. Its old walls, gates, and open spaces create a different atmosphere from the busy streets of Manila. You can walk around the grounds, take photos, appreciate the historic structures, and learn more about Philippine history. It is a good place to visit when you want to spend your free time exploring somewhere meaningful and interesting.

Place Details

Location Sta. Clara St., Intramuros, Manila
Type Historic Fortress and Heritage Site
Entrance Fee ₱75 regular / ₱50 discounted
Best Time to Visit Morning or late afternoon
Recommended Activity Walking, sightseeing, photography, and learning history
